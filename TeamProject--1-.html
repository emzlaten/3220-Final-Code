1. Problem Statement and Background

Problem Definition

1.1 The central focus of this dataset is to provide insights into the factors influencing peoples’ willingness to consider purchasing an electric vehicle (EV). In particular, this dataset includes factors ranging from peoples’ age, income, and daily commute to their range anxiety level, environmental concern level, and whether or not a subsidy is available. 1.2 The target audience may include any of the following:

  • Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs): OEMs have a vested interest in consumer behavior and trends in particular automotive subgroups. Electric vehicles are growing in popularity and charging stations are becoming more prevalent across the United States. This information may assist OEMs in determining the amount of resources they divert towards an electric fleet.

  • Local City Governments: Public charging stations require investment and planning from government. This data and analysis may assist in determining to what degree charging stations should be prioritized for the benefit of their citizens.

  • Electric Utilities: Power companies would benefit from knowing how prevalent EVs are and how much they’re influenced by local charging stations. If charging stations increased, there may be a change in peoples’ willingness to buy EVs, and thus increase usage on these stations.

Data and Exploratory Analysis

The dataset includes 15 features, including numerical, categorical, and character data types. The target feature is “Will_Buy_EV” which states whether an individual will express interest in buying an EV (0 for no, 1 for yes). The data is based on a real world situation but is synthetically-generated.
